Delete your data

How to erase what Zakat Calculator keeps about you.

Zakat Calculator has no account and no sign-in. Your amounts and settings live only on your phone. You can delete everything yourself, in a few taps.

Delete on your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Zakat Calculator → Storage.
  2. Tap Clear data — this erases everything the app stored on your phone.
  3. Or simply uninstall the app, which removes all its data.

Anything else?

We hold no account or financial data about you on our servers. If you emailed us before and want those emails deleted, write to contact@ashbuild.app with the subject "Zakat Calculator data deletion request" and we will delete our copies of your emails within 30 days.
