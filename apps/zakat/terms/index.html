1. What the app does

Zakat Calculator helps you estimate the Zakat you owe on gold, silver, cash, savings and business assets, using the standard 2.5% rate and the Nisab threshold.

2. Estimates, not rulings

Results are estimates for guidance only. They are not a religious ruling (fatwa), legal advice or financial advice. Nisab values depend on live gold and silver prices and on scholarly positions that differ. If you are unsure, ask a qualified scholar.

3. Your responsibility

You are responsible for the amounts you enter and for checking the result before acting on it. We are not liable for any Zakat paid or not paid based on the app's calculations.

4. Acceptable use

Use the app lawfully and do not try to disrupt it, copy it, or pass it off as your own.

5. Availability

The app is provided "as is". Live Nisab prices depend on third-party price sources and may be delayed or unavailable; the app still works offline with the last known prices.
