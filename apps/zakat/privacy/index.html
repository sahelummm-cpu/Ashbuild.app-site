Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

The short version: your financial numbers never leave your phone. There is no account and no sign-in. Zakat Calculator works offline, and we do not collect the amounts you enter.

This policy explains what the Zakat Calculator app ("Zakat Calculator", "we", "us" — a product of Ashbuild) does with your information.

1. Your calculations

All Zakat calculations happen on your device. The gold, silver, cash, savings and business amounts you enter are stored only on your phone, if at all, and are never sent to our servers. We have no way to see them.

2. No account, no personal data

Zakat Calculator does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give us your name, email or phone number. We do not collect personal information directly.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device information such as your advertising ID, device type and approximate location to serve and measure ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Live Nisab prices

To show today's Nisab, the app fetches current gold and silver prices from a public price source. That request contains no personal or financial information about you.

5. Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your phone, you can delete everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you want anything else removed, email contact@ashbuild.app — see our delete-your-data page.

6. Children

Zakat Calculator is a general calculator app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@ashbuild.app.
