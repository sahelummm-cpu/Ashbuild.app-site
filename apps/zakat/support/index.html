How is Zakat calculated?

Zakat is 2.5% of your total zakatable wealth — gold, silver, cash, savings and business assets, minus immediate debts — if it stays above the Nisab for one full lunar year (Hawl). The app does the math for you.

What is Nisab?

Nisab is the minimum wealth that makes Zakat obligatory — 87.48 grams of gold or 612.36 grams of silver. The app converts today's gold and silver prices into your currency so you always know the current Nisab.

Do I pay Zakat on gold jewelry?

Most scholars say yes — gold and silver jewelry above the Nisab is zakatable. Enter your jewelry weight and the app calculates it. When in doubt, ask a qualified scholar.

Is my data private?

Yes. The app works offline and the amounts you enter stay on your phone. See our Privacy Policy.
