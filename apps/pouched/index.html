How it works

Small steps down. Every day doable.

Stopping overnight means fighting full-strength withdrawal all at once. Pouched starts where you are and brings your daily limit down gradually, so your body and habits adjust together.

1

Start from your real number

Tell Pouched how many pouches you use a day, what a can costs and how strong they are. That's your baseline.

2

Pick your pace

Taper over 14, 21, 30, 60 or 90 days, either cutting back to a lower number or stopping completely. Pouched sets today's limit for you.

3

Log, breathe, repeat

Tap once per pouch. When a craving spikes, open a guided tool or ask the coach. Slips are logged too, and your progress doesn't reset.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Pouched free?

Yes. Tracking, your taper plan, savings and the craving tools are free. Pouched Pro is an optional upgrade that unlocks the AI coach, the craving heatmap, plan changes, SOS reminders, the full set of daily missions and the shareable progress report. It's available as a weekly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, and you can cancel subscriptions any time in your store account.

Does it work with my brand?

Yes. Pouched works with any nicotine pouch, snus or dip. You set your own can price, pouches per can and strength, so the numbers match what you actually use.

Do I have to quit completely?

No. When you set up your plan you can aim to stop completely or to cut back to a lower daily number. With Pouched Pro you can change your goal and pace at any time.

What happens if I slip?

Log it. A slip doesn't wipe out your progress. Pouched records what triggered it so you can plan for that moment next time, and your plan keeps going.

Is my data private?

Your tracking data is stored on your device, and there's no account. The only things that leave your phone are purchase records handled by the App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat, and, if you use Ask Pouched, your message and a short progress summary. Read the privacy policy for the full list.

Is Pouched medical advice?

No. Pouched is a wellness and habit-tracking tool. Health timelines in the app are general estimates. If you have questions about nicotine dependence or withdrawal, talk to a doctor or pharmacist. Many people find nicotine replacement or other support helps alongside a plan.
