How it works

Tear. Open. Squeeze!

Every box is a little surprise. Which bun is waiting inside?

1

Tear the pack

Pick a blind bag and swipe along the seam. Crinkly, satisfying, a tiny bit noisy.

2

Open the box

Tap, tap, POP! The lid flies off, the light spills out, and the reveal begins.

3

Squeeze it

Every bun squishes under your finger with its own sound and a soft buzz. Then it moves into its room.

Cozy rooms

Give every bun a home

Each new bun flies into its spot in the series room. Fill all ten and the whole room glows gold.

  • Empty spots show a soft silhouette, so you know who's missing.
  • Find a bun again and it earns a star. Three stars and it turns Shiny.
  • Tap anyone in the room to give them a squeeze.

Questions

Good to know

Is Squishy Bun free?

Yes. The game is free and every box is free. The only purchase is the optional “Remove Ads”, which turns off the banner and the short ads between boxes. Lucky Pack videos stay available because they are always your choice.

When can I play?

Squishy Bun is coming soon to Google Play for Android. Want a heads-up? Email contact@ashbuild.app.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your collection is saved on your phone.

Is it OK for children?

The game asks for a birth year when it first starts. Younger players get child-directed ads only, rated for all ages and never personalised. See our privacy policy for details.

What are Shiny buns?

Find the same bun again and it earns a star. At three stars it becomes Shiny, with a glittery coat and a golden name in your collection.
