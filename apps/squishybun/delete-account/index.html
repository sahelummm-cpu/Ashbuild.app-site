Delete Your Data

Squishy Bun has no accounts. Your collection is saved on your phone, so you can delete it yourself instantly.

Delete the data on your phone

To remove your save (which buns you own, box counters, settings and whether you bought Remove Ads), uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings: Apps → Squishy Bun → Storage → Clear storage.

Because there are no accounts, we hold no game data about you to delete.

Data held by Google

Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google’s policies. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone’s settings, and ask Google about data it holds through your Google account settings.

Subscriptions

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. The only purchase, Remove Ads, is a one-time item, not a subscription. If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as needed to help you, and delete it on request: contact@ashbuild.app.

For the full picture of what we hold and why, read the Privacy Policy.
