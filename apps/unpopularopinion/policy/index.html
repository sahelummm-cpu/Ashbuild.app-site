Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

At Unpopular Opinion, we believe in spicy takes, not in taking your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to your privacy.

1. Information We Don't Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or link your social media profiles.

2. User-Generated Content

Any custom opinions or "takes" you author within the app using the Custom Topics or AI Generator features are stored exclusively on your local device. We never transmit your custom cards to our servers.

3. Voting & Analytics

When you vote on built-in opinions, the app aggregates vote counts anonymously via our backend infrastructure (AWS). These votes are not tied to any user identifier, device ID, or IP address. We only track the total "Agree" and "Disagree" counts to show community consensus.

4. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

We use RevenueCat to manage "Pro" subscriptions and in-app purchases. All transactions are securely processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not have access to your payment details.

5. AI Features

Our AI Generator uses the Gemini API to generate new opinions on the fly. The topic keywords you input are sent to this service strictly for the purpose of generating the cards. They are not stored or linked to your identity.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@ashbuild.app.
