Delete Your Data — Unpopular Opinion

We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information. There is no account to delete.

Delete the data on your phone

Any custom opinions or “takes” you author in the app are stored exclusively on your local device. We never transmit them to our servers. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Votes and purchases

Votes on built-in opinions are counted anonymously and are not tied to any user identifier, device ID, or IP address, so there is nothing to delete. Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play; deleting your data does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in your store subscriptions.
