How to play

Train. Steal. Hatch!

Every egg you bring home hatches into a pet that earns cash for you, even while you're away.

1

Get fast

Run on your treadmill to build Speed. Better treadmills, trails and a bigger base make you faster still.

2

Steal an egg

Tiptoe up to a nest, grab the egg and sprint home before the guardian wakes up and catches you.

3

Hatch & earn

Place it in your pen, watch it crack and meet your new pet. Big, Huge and Golden eggs earn even more.

Questions

Good to know

Is Egg Thief free?

Yes. The game is free. Optional videos give boosts like Turbo x3 or double cash, and you never have to watch them. The only purchase is “Remove Ads”, which turns off the short ads that sometimes play after you place an egg.

Do I need the internet?

No. Egg Thief plays offline. The rival thieves are computer players inside the game. Videos and ads only show when your phone is online.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your farm is saved on your phone.

Is it OK for children?

Yes. Every player gets child-directed ads rated for all ages that are never personalised, and the game doesn't read your phone's advertising ID. See our privacy policy for details.
