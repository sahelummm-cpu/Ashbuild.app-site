Frequently asked questions

How do I use a pose?

Open the library, tap a pose, then fade, flip and line yourself up on the live camera. Tap the shutter to shoot.

Does the saved photo have the overlay on it?

No. The photo is the camera’s full-quality frame, with no overlay and no watermark.

Does it work offline?

Poses you save and your own photos work with no internet. Browsing the rest of the library needs a connection.

I bought Pro but it is locked

Open Settings → Restore purchases while online and signed in to the same Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions and refunds go through Google Play: see your subscriptions and Google Play’s refund help.

How do I delete my data?

PoseBuddy has no account. Uninstalling deletes everything it stored on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
