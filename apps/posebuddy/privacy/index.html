Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026 · Applies to the PoseBuddy app (com.ashbuild.posebuddy) and this website.

PoseBuddy has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your photos stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Photos you take, photos you pick from your gallery as poses, your saved poses and your settings. We do not upload, see or store them. Camera frames are processed only on the device.

What we collect

An anonymous identifier and your subscription status, through RevenueCat (revenuecat.com), only to know whether you have PoseBuddy Pro. We do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Downloads

The app downloads public pose photos from our storage provider, Cloudflare. As with any download, your IP address is visible to that provider.

Permissions

Camera, to show the live view and take photos. Photos/media access, to save your photos to your gallery and to let you choose a gallery photo as a pose. You are told why before each permission is requested.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. The anonymous subscription identifier holds no personal data; to have anything linked to your request deleted, email contact@ashbuild.app.

Children

PoseBuddy is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
