Delete Your Data — PoseBuddy

PoseBuddy has no accounts. Your photos, saved poses and settings stay on your phone. We do not upload, see or store them.

Delete the data on your phone

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.

Anything linked to your subscription

We use an anonymous identifier and your subscription status, through RevenueCat, only to know whether you have PoseBuddy Pro. It holds no personal data. To have anything linked to your request deleted, email contact@ashbuild.app.

Subscriptions

Deleting your data does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions.

For the full picture of what we hold and why, read the Privacy Policy.
