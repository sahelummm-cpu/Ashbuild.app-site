DuoClash

2 player games, 1v1, face to face on one phone.

Put one phone flat between you and clash. Each player owns half of the screen, and all ten games are quick, tactile duels. No second phone, no wifi, no sign-up.

Get it on Google Play Help & FAQ

10 games, each with its own 3D board

  • Air HockeyPhysical puck, 3D mallets and a Smash Shot.
  • Energy Tug of WarTap to pull; grab orbs for 2x Turbo Pulls.
  • Vanishing Tic-Tac-ToeThree pieces each, the oldest vanishes. No ties.
  • Cyber FlashA Western quick-draw. Draw early and you lose.
  • Coin SnatchGrab the gold, dodge the decoy bombs.
  • Cyber TanksOne-touch tanks and ricochet lasers in a brick maze.
  • Neon Paint SplatA 15-second war to paint the most tiles.
  • Sumo PushShove your rival off a shrinking ring.
  • Connect 4Classic four in a row with clattering 3D discs.
  • Color TrapThe Stroop test: the word says BLUE, the ink is red.

Built for two people, one screen

  • True split-screen multi-touchBoth players play at once. The top half is turned around for Player 1, and one player's fingers never block the other's.
  • Tournament CupA best-of-5 across random games, ending with a champion on the podium.
  • Playing alone? Take on the botCasual, Pro or Unbeatable, in every game.
  • Plays offlineEvery game runs on your phone with no connection. Ads, if you have not removed them, load when you are online.

Ads, kept out of the way

The first three sessions have no ads at all. After that there is a small banner on the main menu and, at most, one full-screen ad after a finished Tournament Cup, never more than once every 3.5 minutes and never in the middle of a game. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns them all off for good.
