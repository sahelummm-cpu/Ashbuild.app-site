Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Freed and the tapering method.

How does the gradual reduction method work? ▼
When you set up Freed, you enter your current puff frequency. Freed generates a personalized daily allowance that slowly decreases by 2–5% each day. This gives your brain receptors time to down-regulate dopamine sensitivity naturally, dramatically reducing cravings.
What happens if I exceed my limit on a hard day? ▼
Freed does not punish you. Slip-ups are data, not failures. If you exceed your limit, Freed notes the pattern, prompts you to reflect on triggers, and your limit safely resets the next morning so you can get right back on track.
Is my health and check-in data private? ▼
Yes, 100%. Freed stores all logs, reflections, and metrics locally on your device. We do not sell personal data, track your location, or share your habits with third parties. You can also enable Face ID / Touch ID lock in Settings.
What is the Real-Time Nicotine Detox Simulator? ▼
Freed models the clinical pharmacokinetics of nicotine elimination (biological half-life = 120 minutes) from your last puff. It displays your active systemic blood nicotine level, current half-life stage, and a live countdown to 72 hours — the point where nicotine is 100% eliminated from human tissue.
Can I share my progress with my doctor or therapist? ▼
Yes! Freed provides a 1-tap Clinical Doctor & Accountability Progress Report. It compiles your verified clean hours, puff reduction curves, adherence score, and biological recovery milestones into an exportable summary suitable for medical appointments or sponsor check-ins.
Is Freed free to download? ▼
Yes! Freed is free to download and use for core puff tracking, streak counting, and breathing exercises. We also offer Freed Pro with unlimited AI coaching, craving heatmaps, and advanced quit analytics.
