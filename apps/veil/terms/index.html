Veil makes your screen harder to read for people beside you. It is a helpful layer, not a guarantee of privacy.

These terms cover your use of the Veil: Privacy Screen app ("Veil", "we", "us" — a product of Ashbuild). By installing or using Veil you agree to them.

1. Who can use Veil

Veil is for people 18 or older. If you are under 18, please do not use it.

2. What Veil does and does not do

  • Veil dims your screen, applies a pattern or tint, and can cover the screen when your phone tilts, when you look away, or when a second face looks at it.
  • It is not privacy glass. Someone close to you, at the right angle, in a bright room, or using a camera may still be able to read your screen.
  • Do not rely on Veil as your only protection for sensitive information. Use a screen lock and take care in public.
  • To keep touches working on Android 12 and newer, the touch-through filter is limited by Android to a maximum darkness.
  • Camera modes depend on lighting and on the phone, and can miss a face or see one that is not there.
  • Camera modes use more battery. Veil has a battery saver setting for them.

  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. Restore purchases in Settings brings Pro back on a new phone with the same Google account.
  • Free features, including the Dim Filter, Tilt Guard and Instant Cover, have no time limits.
