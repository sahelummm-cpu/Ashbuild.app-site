Delete Your Data

Veil has no account and no server of ours. Your settings and activity counts stay on your phone.

Delete the data on your phone

Uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings to remove everything it keeps on your phone: settings, activity counts and your Auto-on app list.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete.

Data held by Google

For anything Google holds about you (purchases, ads), use your Google Account settings.

Subscriptions

Deleting your data does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel Veil Pro any time in Google Play → Subscriptions. Ask us anything at contact@ashbuild.app.

For the full picture of what we hold and why, read the Privacy Policy.
