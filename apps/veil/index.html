Keep your screen to yourself

Veil dims your screen and covers it the moment your phone tilts, you look away, or someone else looks in. It makes your screen harder to read for people beside you.

Coming soon to Google PlayPrivacy policy

For adults aged 18 and over. Android 8.0 or newer.

Dim Filter

A darkening layer over any app, with tints and a fine pattern. Your touches still go straight through.

Tilt Guard

Covers the screen when your phone tips past the angle you choose, and uncovers it when you level it again.

Instant Cover

One tap to black out the screen. Tap twice to bring it back.

Look-Away PRO

Uses the front camera to cover the screen when you look away from it.

Peek Alert PRO

Covers the screen when a second face looks at it, and counts the glances blocked.

Private by design

Camera pictures are checked in memory and thrown away. Nothing is saved or uploaded. Core protection never shows an ad.
